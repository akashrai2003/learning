# Coverage of the original curricula

P = PyTorch (62 modules); I = inference (48 modules); E = edge (24 weeks); C = career (16 weeks). Every one of these 150 units is accounted for below. Original support guides and projects remain in [the source library](originals/README.md).

**Focused** means the combined schedule assigns a specific implementation, measurement or source exercise. It does not mean every original exercise is required or the whole module is mastered. **Survey** means selective reading, a small example or source inspection; deep implementation remains follow-up work. **Deferred** means no required session in this plan.

This is the scope that makes 663 hours plausible. All original lessons remain available; completing them all in depth is a longer program.
