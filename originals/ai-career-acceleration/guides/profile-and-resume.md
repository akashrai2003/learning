# Resume, profile and interview narrative

## Two focused resume variants

Keep a common factual record and change emphasis. The applied AI/backend version should lead with delivered systems, retrieval/extraction quality, APIs, queues and deployment. The platform/inference version should lead with serving, measurement, GPU memory, operational debugging and reproducible infrastructure. Neither should invent skills or alter dates.

Use a clean readable format, typically one page at your experience level unless substantial relevant evidence justifies a second. Include standard section names, visible text links and ordinary bullets. Check that the exported PDF is selectable and text extraction preserves meaning. No particular formatting guarantees an ATS pass.

## Evidence-based bullets

Use: action + owned component + constraint + validation/outcome. Draft examples below require fact-checking and disclosure approval; they are not ready-made claims.

- 'Built and deployed an on-prem document intelligence workflow spanning ingestion, storage, retrieval and model-serving integration; owned [specific components] and [release/recovery responsibilities].'
- 'Investigated inference memory and throughput limits on [publicly shareable hardware/model]; changed [specific configuration/code] and measured [metric] under [workload].'
- 'Developed extraction review and evaluation workflows for complex documents, separating model disagreement from confirmed errors through [method].'
- 'Implemented [queue/retry/checkpoint behavior] to recover from [failure], validated using [test or incident evidence].'

Replace brackets with verified details. Do not turn configuration tuning into a claim that you implemented the serving runtime. Do not describe offline experiment throughput as production traffic. If a result is a team outcome, identify your contribution accurately.

## Metrics audit

For every number ask: what was measured; where the raw evidence is; baseline and final configuration; workload; denominator; single-request versus aggregate; quality or reliability cost; permission to publish. If the answer is weak, remove the number or qualify it. A strong nonnumeric bullet is preferable to a misleading one.

## About section working draft

'I build production AI systems that connect document processing, backend services and local model inference. My work has included on-prem deployment, ingestion and retrieval workflows, evaluation, and debugging model-serving behavior under resource constraints.

I am now building public, reproducible experiments around inference latency, memory and deployment reliability, while developing C++ and edge-runtime skills. I am interested in applied AI and AI platform roles where I can own systems beyond the prototype stage. Based in Bengaluru, I am open to India roles and global teams able to hire remotely in India.'

Edit this into your voice and add approved concrete evidence. Do not publish it automatically. Avoid implying that all current work is public or open source.

## Six interview stories

Prepare two-minute and eight-minute versions of: hardest production failure; performance tradeoff; quality/evaluation failure; ownership under ambiguity; disagreement or collaboration; and a mistake you corrected. Use context, responsibility, choices, action, validation and lesson. Keep enough technical detail to withstand follow-up.

Your inference model-switching investigation can support a strong story if you can explain which process owned weights and cache, what happened on sleep/wake, how you detected behavior, alternatives considered and the actual fix. Do not repeat a remembered metric without recovering the test conditions.

## Strong introduction

In 60–90 seconds: current role and scope; one production system you owned; one concrete engineering problem; why this team fits your next step. Salary, full tool lists and a complete autobiography are not needed. For a remote team, mention written documentation and independent debugging as demonstrated habits rather than personality claims.

## Handling gaps

Say 'I have operated and investigated this runtime; my source-level contribution is [specific work]' instead of claiming deep kernel expertise. For Kubernetes or distributed inference gaps, explain the closest system you built and one focused exercise you completed. Acknowledge that a single GPU does not establish multi-node production experience.

## References and consistency

Ask potential references privately and only when needed; this pack sends no messages. Use the same job dates and role names across resume, LinkedIn and forms. Verify full-time versus internship transition dates. Current compensation of approximately 20 LPA is an input to negotiation planning, not a public headline or a market-value ceiling.
