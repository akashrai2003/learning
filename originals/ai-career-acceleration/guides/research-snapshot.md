## How to extend the list

Search employers building inference APIs, GPU clouds, evaluation/observability, enterprise document AI, AI developer tools and production agents. Use official role pages, not social salary rumors. Add one company only when you can write why your evidence fits, where the role is, and which hiring constraints remain unknown.

It is reasonable to watch ambitious US/EU infrastructure companies even when there is no India role today. Keep these in a watchlist, not the active eligible-application funnel. A foreign headquarters is not the same thing as a foreign salary available in India.
