# Finding and qualifying opportunities

## Search for the work, not only the title

Use several title families: AI Engineer, Applied AI Engineer, Backend Engineer AI, ML Platform Engineer, Inference Platform Engineer, LLM Infrastructure Engineer, AI Deployment Engineer and Developer Tools Engineer. 'GenAI Engineer' can retrieve useful work but also many low-depth integration roles. Read actual responsibilities.

Start with direct company career pages, LinkedIn Jobs, YC Jobs and Wellfound. Search engines can discover ATS postings, but apply through a verified official destination. See the dated [research snapshot](research-snapshot.md) for inspected examples and limitations.

## Search query bank

Run short focused searches and record what worked:

```text
("AI Engineer" OR "Applied AI Engineer") (India OR worldwide OR APAC) remote
("inference" OR "ML platform") engineer (India OR Bangalore OR Bengaluru)
("vLLM" OR "SGLang") (hiring OR careers OR engineer)
("LLM" OR "AI") "remote" "contractor" "India"
site:jobs.ashbyhq.com "AI" "India"
site:job-boards.greenhouse.io "inference" "India"
site:jobs.lever.co "machine learning" "remote"
site:ycombinator.com/companies "AI" "Remote" "India"
```

Query syntax varies by search engine and job board. Use 'posted recently' filters when available, but verify the actual listing; search crawl dates are not posting dates. Avoid excluding 'Senior' globally: some companies use it loosely while others attach a strict experience requirement.

## Eligibility gate before application effort

1. Is the role still present on the official site?
2. Does it allow working from India? What exact wording establishes that?
3. Is employment via an India entity, EOR or contractor route available, or still unconfirmed?
4. Is the experience requirement approximately aligned, flexible, or a hard mismatch?
5. Is the role's work compatible with your direction and schedule?
6. Is pay published, recruiter-confirmed, unknown, or clearly below your target?
7. Is the needed timezone overlap sustainable from Bengaluru?

Record eligibility as **confirmed India / explicitly worldwide but arrangement unconfirmed / unclear / excluded**. 'EU remote' does not pass the India gate. If a role is attractive but unclear, send one precise question before a long take-home assignment.

## A practical fit score

Use a 100-point local prioritization score: responsibility overlap 30; demonstrable evidence 25; level fit 15; location/arrangement clarity 15; compensation feasibility 10; personal constraints 5. Hard exclusions such as mandatory US residency override the total. This score is a planning tool, not a calibrated hiring prediction.

Suggested buckets: A for clear fit with strong evidence, B for adjacent or one resolved gap, C for selective stretch, and no-apply for hard exclusions. Spend most effort on A/B. A known employer with a glamorous title is not automatically A.

## Build a target list that stays useful

Keep around 20–30 company/team targets rather than hundreds of unreviewed bookmarks. For each: product, why your experience matters, relevant team, source URL, actual role if available, geography, level, pay status, and next action. Separate a **company watchlist** from **verified open role**. A company without an eligible role can remain a research target but should not inflate your application count.

Refresh highest-priority roles weekly. Archive closed roles and retain the reason; do not silently convert them into still-open recommendations. If an employer has a careers alert, set it up manually if you want it. This pack creates no scheduled alerts or recurring tasks.

## Application packet

For each serious role prepare: the appropriate resume, a two- or three-sentence fit explanation, one relevant public evidence link, and a short gap explanation when needed. A model-serving role should see the benchmark/incident case first; a document AI role should see evaluation and extraction evidence first.

Apply through the official process, then optionally send a relevant human a concise note with the role and evidence. A referral is helpful but not required to begin. Do not send multiple inconsistent applications to the same organization without tracking them.

## Recruiter qualification call

Ask what the team owns; level and experience flexibility; India eligibility and arrangement; currency and base/variable/equity split; overlap hours; interview stages; assessment expectations; expected start date; and whether your notice period is workable. Ask for the budget range early enough to avoid a large mismatched process. If a recruiter cannot answer, mark it unconfirmed rather than inventing a number.

## Remote roles versus AI evaluation projects

Coding-evaluation and model-training platforms can provide paid projects, but verify work scope, guaranteed hours, duration, review/payment rules and continuity. A high hourly headline is not a permanent engineering salary. Do not count an invitation or platform profile as an offer. Consider such projects selectively if they build relevant skills and do not interfere with your employment obligations.

## Review the funnel

If 15–20 well-qualified applications produce no conversations, inspect resume evidence, level and geography before sending 100 more. If screens happen but technical rounds fail, shift time to the specific failure category. If final rounds happen without offers, review communication, scope, references and compensation alignment. Small samples are noisy; treat these thresholds as prompts for diagnosis, not statistical proof.
