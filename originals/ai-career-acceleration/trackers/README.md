# Tracker instructions

These CSV files begin empty. They are schemas for your real activity, not proof of applications or fabricated contacts. Open them in a spreadsheet or editor. Keep private contact, interview and offer details out of public repositories.

Start with opportunities and applications. Add the other trackers only as activity occurs. Unknown fields remain blank or explicitly unknown. Use consistent company and role identifiers so conversations do not inflate application counts. Track actual source dates separately from job posting dates.

Fit score is a prioritization aid defined in the job-search guide, not a hiring probability. The public status of a contribution must distinguish local, submitted, under review, merged and closed. The post tracker uses platform analytics only where available. Offer values must keep recurring cash separate from one-time bonuses and speculative equity.

Review by cohort and allow response lag. Do not optimize a conversion rate by deleting rejected or failed records.
