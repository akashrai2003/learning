## Daily assignments

### Day 078 — Story audit

**Optional date:** 2027-01-08 • **Core time:** 150 minutes.

**Technical / preparation block:** Spend 90 minutes rehearsing two-minute/eight-minute versions of three work stories.

**Evidence / market block:** Use 30 minutes verifying metrics and 30 on active process next steps.

**Complete when:** Personal responsibility, alternatives and validation are explicit; uncertain numbers are removed.

**Log:** save the code/test/report or application record and a short note: what improved, what remains blocked, and the next smallest action. Link actual evidence; do not invent activity to fill a tracker.

**Optional extra 30 minutes:** repair one failed check or rehearse the weakest upcoming interview concept. If all checks pass, stop or read a targeted section from the earlier C++/inference curriculum. Do not open another project.
