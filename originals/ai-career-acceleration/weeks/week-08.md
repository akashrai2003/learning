### Day 050 — Second coding baseline

**Optional date:** 2026-12-11 • **Core time:** 150 minutes.

**Technical / preparation block:** Spend 60 minutes on a mixed timed problem and 30 comparing with week-one weaknesses.

**Evidence / market block:** Use 60 minutes on active applications/screens.

**Complete when:** Report independent improvement and remaining gaps without changing the scoring standard.

**Log:** save the code/test/report or application record and a short note: what improved, what remains blocked, and the next smallest action. Link actual evidence; do not invent activity to fill a tracker.
