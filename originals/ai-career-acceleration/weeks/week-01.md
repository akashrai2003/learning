# Week 01: Positioning and evidence inventory

Seven 150-minute core sessions: 17.5 hours total. Each day has an optional 30-minute extension. The allocations below replace the full daily workload of the earlier inference pack during this career sprint; they are not added on top.

## Daily assignments

### Day 001 — Define the next role

**Optional date:** 2026-10-23 • **Core time:** 150 minutes.

**Technical / preparation block:** Spend 60 minutes diagnosing Python coding with one map/array task; explain complexity and edge cases.

**Evidence / market block:** Spend 60 minutes inventorying six work stories and 30 choosing two role families; use the current 20 LPA and approximate two-YoE baseline.

**Complete when:** Save a role thesis and factual timeline; separate internships, full-time work and overlapping freelance periods.

**Log:** save the code/test/report or application record and a short note: what improved, what remains blocked, and the next smallest action. Link actual evidence; do not invent activity to fill a tracker.

**Optional extra 30 minutes:** repair one failed check or rehearse the weakest upcoming interview concept. If all checks pass, stop or read a targeted section from the earlier C++/inference curriculum. Do not open another project.
