**Optional date:** 2026-10-30 • **Core time:** 150 minutes.

**Technical / preparation block:** Use 60 minutes defining load, prefill, decode and application TTFT in the project.

**Evidence / market block:** Spend 60 minutes implementing a small benchmark invocation and 30 qualifying two roles.

**Complete when:** Raw output is preserved; timing definitions do not mix runtime-only and end-to-end metrics.

**Log:** save the code/test/report or application record and a short note: what improved, what remains blocked, and the next smallest action. Link actual evidence; do not invent activity to fill a tracker.
