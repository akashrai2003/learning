### Day 036 — Review response

**Optional date:** 2026-11-27 • **Core time:** 150 minutes.

**Technical / preparation block:** Spend 90 minutes addressing actual reviewer feedback or completing missing local checks.

**Evidence / market block:** Use 30 minutes qualifying roles and 30 on a relevant application.

**Complete when:** Changes answer the technical concern; if waiting, improve owned project instead of repeatedly bumping.

**Log:** save the code/test/report or application record and a short note: what improved, what remains blocked, and the next smallest action. Link actual evidence; do not invent activity to fill a tracker.
