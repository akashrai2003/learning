# AI engineering career acceleration: 16 weeks

Prepared for Akash Rai • Research snapshot: 22 October 2026

## Your targets

- Primary international stretch: **USD 80,000–100,000 annual gross compensation or contract revenue**, working remotely from India for a US/EU or globally hiring organization.
- Parallel domestic target: **INR 35–40 lakh per year**, with Bengaluru and India-remote roles both eligible.
- Current baseline supplied for this plan: approximately **INR 20 LPA and two years of experience**. This overrides older salary information. Confirm exact employment dates, fixed/variable breakdown and notice period before using applications.
- Desired movement: an offer within approximately 3–4 months. Interviews and offer negotiation begin before the learning curriculum is finished.

These are targets, not salary-survey conclusions or promised outcomes. A global employer does not automatically offer US-local compensation to an India-based hire. A contractor's gross revenue is not the same package as employee base plus benefits. The plan improves evidence, targeting and interview readiness; it cannot guarantee a salary or hiring timeline.

## The central strategy

Sell demonstrated production AI ownership now: document intelligence, backend engineering, local model serving, deployment reliability and measured inference improvements. Build runtime depth through a tightly scoped public project and one relevant open-source contribution lane. Use LinkedIn to make this work discoverable. Keep C++ as a compounding specialization rather than postponing applications until you can compete for kernel-specialist roles.

Your strongest initial role family is **AI Engineer / Applied AI Engineer / AI Platform Engineer / Backend Engineer for AI systems**, with selected inference-serving roles where Python, debugging, benchmarking and operational ownership matter. CUDA kernel and compiler roles remain a selective longer-term lane until you have direct evidence.

## Research and action boundary

Official career pages, contribution guidelines and LinkedIn sources were inspected for this guide. Hiring conditions, issue status and repository policies change. Recheck primary links before applying or beginning work. Sample issue numbers are scouting leads, not reserved or guaranteed-open work. No application, outreach, post, issue or PR has been sent for you.

The guide does not publish client material. Public examples should be independently reproduced with public models and synthetic/public documents. Verify what you can disclose from employment work, including code ownership and metrics, before publication.
